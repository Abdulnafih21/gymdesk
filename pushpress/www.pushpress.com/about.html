Gym Software Built By Gym Owners

Hi! We're PushPress!

Our mission is to make gym management the easiest part of running a fitness business. PushPress was built to challenge outdated systems and replace them with software that’s simple, powerful, and designed to help gyms thrive. Every feature we build aims to deliver best-in-class service and lasting impact, making PushPress a trusted partner for thousands of gyms worldwide.Today, we’re building an AI-powered system that makes gym management the easiest part of running a business.

Meet the Founders

Dan Uyemura

CEO

A lifelong fitness enthusiast, Dan has spent his entire career working in startups. From 2010 to 2012, he co-owned LAX CrossFit before opening Torrance Training Lab, a thriving gym he operated until 2019. After selling the business, Dan shifted his focus to growing PushPress — helping other gym owners streamline operations and achieve lasting success.
