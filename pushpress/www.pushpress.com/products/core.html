Got Questions? We got answers.

What is gym management software?

Gym management software allows you to easily manage your fitness studio from one central place. The system allows you to track all of your revenue, schedule your classes, and manage your payroll.

What kind of features should a gym management software have?

Gym management software can range from very simple to very complex. But every software should have the following basic features:
- Automated billing
- Digital contracts and waivers
- Easy online sales

Why do you need gym management software? 

Gym management software allows you to streamline the management of your business. You’ll have a single destination for member management, scheduling, payroll, lead management, and documentation. Without a software system, you would be using up valuable time taking care of tasks that software can do for you. 

How much does gym management software cost?

Gym management software costs can vary. But across all software systems, you should evaluate the price of the software (recurring costs) and the transaction costs. PushPress has a Free option, built for gyms who are just starting. 

Who are gym management systems for? 

Gym management software is for anyone that has a fitness business. The system you use will depend on the size of your gym. But businesses that can benefit from a gym management system include Crossfit affiliates, bootcamps, yoga studios, spin studios and more. 

How does gym management software work? 

Gym membership systems start by digitizing your club classes and membership plans. From there, the member experience is tracked through the software, allowing you to see and track everything.
