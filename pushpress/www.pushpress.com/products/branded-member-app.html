Got Questions? We got answers.

How long do I have to wait until my branded app is live in the app stores?

We can typically get your app Live in both Android and Apple Store in about 7-10 business days after we receive your logo and home image assets.

What do I need to provide you?

Just your logo and a background image for the home screen. We will pull the rest of the information we need from your PushPress account.

Why the monthly fee?

App updates. We will continually work to improve your app and add new features.

Is there a free trial?

No, you will get charged when we start this process. Its an insane amount of work for us to build and submit your Branded App, so unfortunately there are no freebies

How customized is the app to my business?

We can personalize your app’s icon, device splash screen, colors, menu items, home screen background and of course your app store listing page.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee. Crazy I know since we do all the design and build work upfront for you!

Is there a Contract?

We understand others will lock you into a year contract for a Branded App, but we don't! We know you don't like contracts, all that is required to cancel is a 30 day notice.

Apple Developer Account ($99/yr)

You will need an Apple Developer Account for this app. We can get started without your Apple Developer Account setup, but you will need one. Cost is on you at $99/year.

The good news is that the Android Play Store doesn’t require you to have your own account for this, its only Apple that is making our lives difficult here 😉
