Recurring Appointments

Effortlessly schedule weekly and monthly sessions without upfront credits or endless manual booking. Your members stay engaged, your staff saves time, and your gym runs smoother than ever.
Enable Recurring Appointments

Stop the scheduling scramble

Running a gym means juggling countless moving parts, but member scheduling shouldn't drain your energy. With recurring appointments, you can eliminate the tedious cycle of manually booking the same sessions week after week while removing barriers that keep members from committing to regular training.

Streamlined booking

Schedule recurring sessions directly from your PushPress Core or Staff App without requiring members to purchase credits upfront.

Slash administrative workload

No more manual entry for each individual recurring session — set it once and let the system handle the rest.

Keep members engaged with their fitness journey

Members love seeing their training sessions mapped out in their app, creating accountability and motivation that keeps them coming back and progressing toward their goals.

FAQ

Everything you need to know about Recurring Appointments

Got questions? We’ve got answers.

  • Can I set different times for different days of the week?

    Absolutely! Our flexible system allows you to create recurring appointments with varying times for each day, accommodating the unique schedules of both trainers and members.

  • Do members need to purchase credits before booking recurring sessions?

    Not anymore! Staff can now schedule recurring appointments without requiring members to have pre-purchased credits, making it easier for members to commit to regular sessions.

  • Will this work for both personal training and group classes?

    Yes! Recurring appointments work perfectly for personal training sessions, group fitness classes, and any other regular programming your gym offers.

  • Is this available on the Staff App?

    Yes! Staff can create recurring bookings through Core or Staff App, ensuring the set-up is as easy as possible.
