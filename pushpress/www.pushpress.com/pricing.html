FAQ

Have questions? We have answers.

  • Pricing and Plans

    What does PushPress cost and what’s included at each level?

    PushPress offers multiple pricing tiers:

    • Core (Free): Basic scheduling, billing, and member tracking
    • Core Pro ($159/month): Advanced reporting, automations, lower processing fees
    • Train (starting at $79/month): Workout tracking for up to 20 clients
    • Grow ($329/month): Full CRM with automated workflows, email/SMS, and websites
    • Branded App (Add-on): A white-labeled mobile app for $81–$97/month
    • Full Stack (~$559/month): All features combined into one comprehensive package

  • Payments and Processing

    What payment types does PushPress support?

    You can accept:

    • Recurring membership payments
    • One-time sales and drop-ins
    • PT sessions and class packs
    • Point-of-sale retail payments

    PushPress supports credit cards, ACH, and international transactions. Processing rates are lower with Core Pro.

    ‍

  • Gym Management and Member Features

    What member management features are included?

    PushPress Core features include:

    • Digital waivers
    • Automated billing
    • Member profiles with custom fields
    • Class scheduling
    • QR code check-ins
    • Attendance tracking
    • App-based booking
    • Workout history and logging
    Can I use PushPress to schedule classes?

    Yes. PushPress includes a powerful class scheduler:

    • Recurring class templates
    • Capacity management and waitlists
    • Coach substitutions
    • No-show/late cancel automation
    • Multiple calendar views
    • Kiosk mode for member check-ins
    Does PushPress have built-in marketing tools?

    Yes. PushPress Grow is our marketing and CRM system. It includes:

    • Automated emails and text messages
    • Lead capture forms and landing pages
    • Social media integrations
    • WhatsApp messaging (approved templates included)
    • Member announcements and engagement workflows

    ‍

  • Integrations and Technology

    What third-party tools integrate with PushPress?

    PushPress connects with:

    • Stripe (payments)
    • Mailchimp (email)
    • HybridAF (door access)
    • Social platforms
    • Custom websites
    • Public API access for advanced integrations
    How does the Branded App work?

    The Branded App is a fully white-labeled mobile experience:

    • Your branding and logo
    • Member check-ins and class booking
    • Workout tracking and performance history
    • Push notifications and a community feed
    What can I do with the PushPress Staff App?

    The Staff App supports:

    • Class and check-in management
    • Member profiles and injury/health notes
    • Internal team messaging
    • Retail point-of-sale
    • Appointment scheduling
    • Program delivery
    • Class announcements
    • Staff permission controls

  • Onboarding and Support

    How long does it take to set up PushPress?

    Most gyms go live in a few days. Our onboarding includes:

    1. Setup and data import
    2. Staff training
    3. Member communication support
    4. Live launch assistance
    5. Follow-up optimization and support
    What type of support does PushPress offer?

    You’ll get:

    • Dedicated onboarding specialists
    • Live chat and email support
    • Zoom troubleshooting when needed
    • Video tutorials and help docs
    • Community forums and regular check-ins

    ‍

  • Reporting

    What reporting tools does PushPress include?

    You can track:

    • Revenue and sales performance
    • Member retention and growth
    • Attendance trends and class performance
    • Personal training sessions
    • Staff engagement
    • Exportable reports and custom views

  • Niche Gym Features

    Does PushPress work for CrossFit gyms and martial arts academies?

    Yes. We support:

    • CrossFit-specific programming and benchmarks
    • Martial arts belt and rank tracking
    • Strength and conditioning workouts
    • Group class and personal training
    • Custom program templates and assessments
    • Competition and leaderboard tools
    How does workout tracking work with PushPress Train?

    Train gives coaches and members tools for:

    • Logging daily workouts
    • Creating custom programs
    • Tracking benchmarks and PRs
    • Viewing past performance
    • Coach feedback
    • Optional leaderboards in the app
